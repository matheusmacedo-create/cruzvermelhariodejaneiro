The Brazilian Red Cross — Rio de Janeiro Branch is the Rio de Janeiro State branch of the Brazilian Red Cross, the National Society of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement in Brazil. Its work is carried out largely by volunteers trained by the branch itself, from its own headquarters in the centre of Rio de Janeiro.

Questions and answers

Before you donate

Can I donate from outside Brazil?
Yes. This page takes credit card donations from any country, in English, without a Brazilian tax number (CPF). You choose an amount, enter your details and your card, and the receipt arrives by e-mail. PIX, Brazil's instant payment system, only works between accounts at Brazilian banks, so from abroad the card is the way to give.
Which currency will I be charged in?
In Brazilian reais (R$). Your card issuer converts the amount to your own currency, at its own exchange rate, and may add a foreign transaction fee. The amounts in US dollars, euros and pounds shown on this page are approximations based on the Central Bank of Brazil's reference rate, so the figure on your statement may differ slightly.
Why do you ask for an ID number?
The payment company in Brazil requires an identification number for every charge. Brazilians use their CPF; from abroad you can use your passport number or national ID number, as long as it has digits only. If your passport number has letters, use another government-issued ID that is all digits (a national ID, tax number or driving licence number), or write to us and we will help. The number is used to issue the charge and for the branch's accounting records, and is never made public.
Is it safe? Who processes the payment?
The page is on the branch's official domain, over an encrypted connection. Card donations are processed by ÚnicoPag, a Brazilian payment company. Your card details pass through our server only on their way to ÚnicoPag, at the moment of payment, and are not stored by us. How we handle your data is set out in the Privacy Policy.
My card was declined. What can I do?
Some banks block a first charge from another country as a precaution. Approve the charge in your banking app or call your bank, then try again, or use another credit card. If it still does not go through, write to contato@cruzvermelhariodejaneiro.org and we will help in English.
Will I get a receipt? Is my donation tax-deductible?
You receive a receipt by e-mail as soon as the payment is confirmed, with the amount, the date, a reference number and the branch's Brazilian registration number (CNPJ). The receipt is issued in Brazil. Whether a donation to a Brazilian non-profit can be deducted in your country depends on your local tax rules, and we cannot confirm that for you.
Can I give every month?
From this page, donations are one-off for now. Monthly giving is available on the Portuguese donation page for donors with a Brazilian tax number (CPF). If you would like to give regularly from abroad, write to us and we will arrange it with you.
I live in Brazil or have a CPF. Which page should I use?
Use the Portuguese donation page: with a CPF you can give by PIX or credit card, once or every month. The form is short, and you can write to us in English if anything is unclear.
Can my company or National Society support the branch?
Yes. Sister National Societies, institutions and companies wishing to partner, donate institutionally or sponsor a campaign can write to contato@cruzvermelhariodejaneiro.org. We answer in English.
