## Use of content

The texts and news published here may be shared and quoted freely, with credit and a link to the original page. Photographs may show people assisted by the branch and volunteers: do not reuse them outside their original context without our written permission.

## The red cross emblem

The red cross on a white background is an emblem protected by the Geneva Conventions and by Brazilian law. Its use is restricted — it may not be reproduced on other websites, materials or products without authorisation, even for non-commercial purposes.

## Donations and courses

Donations and course enrolments take place on their own pages, linked from this site, with their official support channels. Be wary of any charge made in the name of the Red Cross outside the official channels — and let us know at the e-mail address above.

## Liability

We work to keep the information accurate and up to date, but it is provided for information purposes. Links to third-party websites are offered in good faith; their content is the responsibility of those who maintain them.
