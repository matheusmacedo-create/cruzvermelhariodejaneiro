## What we collect, and why

### Enrolling in an in-person course

On the enrolment page you give your full name, CPF (Brazilian taxpayer number), e-mail and phone number, choose the course and pay the enrolment fee by PIX or card. We use this data to record the enrolment, issue the charge, send the receipt by e-mail and for the secretariat to contact you, also by e-mail, to confirm the group and timetable.

Payment is processed by ÚnicoPag, the payment company that receives your name, CPF, e-mail and phone number to generate the charge. Card details only pass through our server on their way to ÚnicoPag, at the moment of payment, and are not stored: we keep only the card brand and the last four digits, which appear on the receipt.

Once payment is confirmed, your name, CPF, e-mail and phone number are also sent to the school platform (escola.cursoscruzvermelha.org), when the link with it is active, to create your enrolment and your access to the course.

### Donations

On the donation page you give your full name, CPF, e-mail and phone number, choose the amount and pay by PIX or card. This is the identification data that ÚnicoPag, which processes the payment, requires to issue the charge, and the data we use to send the donation receipt by e-mail. Card details likewise only pass through our server on their way to ÚnicoPag and are not stored (only the card brand and the last four digits are kept).

If you mark your donation as anonymous, your name does not appear in public acknowledgements, on social media or in donor lists. The data is still requested, because the charge and the receipt depend on it, and it stays only with the team that handles donations.

### The "Fale com a gente" chat

The chat, in the corner of the Portuguese pages, asks for the subject, the course (when the subject is enrolment, a course or payment), your name, your e-mail, a phone number (optional) and your message, and records the page you wrote from. The message goes to the team by e-mail, you receive a copy with a reference number, and the reply arrives by e-mail. This data is used only to answer your enquiry.

### Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter on the home page, we keep the name and e-mail you gave and the record of your consent: the text you accepted, the date, the IP address and the browser identification used when subscribing. The subscription only takes effect after you confirm it through the link sent to your e-mail (the link is valid for 72 hours). We use the address only to send news, campaigns and information about courses, as stated in the consent.

Every mailing includes a one-click unsubscribe link. When you unsubscribe, you stop receiving it and we keep only the record that you left, so that you are not subscribed again by mistake. If you ask for deletion, the record is erased.

### Browsing: Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read, and the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) to measure the reach of our outreach campaigns. Both tools use cookies and identifiers in your browser and receive data about the visit, such as the page opened. Google Analytics is set up to treat moving from this site to the school platform (escola.cursoscruzvermelha.org) as a single visit. The two companies' policies are at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [facebook.com/privacy/policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy).

In the chat, at enrolment and in donations, we record steps in these tools such as opening the chat, starting an enrolment or completing a payment, with the subject or the course and the amount — never your name, CPF, e-mail, phone number or message.

### Security and source records

When you submit an enrolment, a donation or a chat message, we record the IP address it came from and, if you arrived through an advertisement or campaign link, the source shown in the page address (the utm, fbclid and gclid parameters). The IP address is used to limit repeated attempts and protect the forms against bots; the source, to know where enrolments, donations and enquiries come from.

### Volunteering

Volunteer registration is done on the volunteering form linked from the site, which is hosted by the Spotform service (form.spotform.com.br), and the volunteering team uses the data provided to get in touch. The branch's only WhatsApp number is that team's, +55 (21) 97036-0264, used only for volunteering matters. All other contact is through the chat and by e-mail.

## Who the data is shared with

- **ÚnicoPag**, which processes enrolment and donation payments: name, CPF, e-mail and phone number and, for card payments, the card details.
- **The school platform** (escola.cursoscruzvermelha.org), for paid enrolments: name, CPF, e-mail and phone number, to create the enrolment and access to the course.
- **Spotform**, which hosts the volunteer registration form.
- **Google and Meta**, through the measurement tools described above.
- **E-mail delivery**: the newsletter is sent through the Resend delivery service. Receipts and chat confirmations are also sent through Resend or, if it is unavailable, through the server that hosts the site.

We do not sell personal data, to anyone, under any circumstances.

## What we do not do

- We do not ask for sensitive data (such as health, religion or racial origin) in any form on the site.
- We do not send the newsletter to anyone who has not confirmed their subscription, and any consent can be withdrawn.
- We do not store card details.

## Cookies and browser storage

The cookies on this site are those of the measurement tools mentioned above. You can block them in your browser settings — the site keeps working normally without them.

The chat stores the progress of the conversation, and the enrolment and donation pages store the source of the visit, in the browser's session storage, which is cleared when the tab is closed. The enrolment page also stores a marker in the browser so that the same payment is not counted twice in the statistics.

## Your rights (article 18 of the LGPD)

You may ask at any time for: confirmation that we process your data; access to it; correction of incomplete, inaccurate or out-of-date data; anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data; portability; information about who we share your data with; and withdrawal of consent, with deletion of the data processed on that basis. Just write to [contato@cruzvermelhariodejaneiro.org](mailto:contato@cruzvermelhariodejaneiro.org) — we reply through the same channel. Data linked to payments may have to be kept to meet legal obligations even after a deletion request. You may also lodge a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

## Retention and security

Data from the forms is kept in systems with access restricted to the branch team responsible for each matter. Browsing statistics are kept on Google's and Meta's platforms, under their policies. We keep data for as long as necessary for the purposes of this policy — answering your enquiry, carrying out the enrolment or donation, sending the newsletter for as long as you want to receive it — and to meet legal obligations.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Any significant change in how newsletter data is handled is announced to subscribers by e-mail.
